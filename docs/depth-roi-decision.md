# Depth ROI decision

Scoring uses 1 (low) to 5 (high). Cost and risk are reverse-scored, so a high
number is favorable.

| Differentiator | User impact | Demo clarity | Reliable now | Clacky support | PointPilot reuse | Cost | Risk | Total |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Continuous voice follow-ups | 5 | 5 | 4 | 2 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 29 |
| Barge-in while speaking | 5 | 5 | 4 | 2 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 29 |
| Spoken correction while acting | 5 | 5 | 4 | 2 | 5 | 3 | 3 | 27 |
| More accurate visual pointing | 4 | 4 | 3 | 5 | 3 | 3 | 3 | 25 |
| Teach -> Guide -> Act | 5 | 5 | 4 | 3 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 30 |
| Result verification | 5 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 5 | 3 | 3 | 25 |
| Compact companion UI | 4 | 5 | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 5 | 31 |
| GIMP-specific depth | 3 | 4 | 3 | 2 | 5 | 2 | 2 | 21 |

## Decision

**Primary differentiation:** one continuous, interruptible voice session.

**Supporting capabilities:** Teach -> Guide -> Act mode transitions and spoken
correction while computer control is active.

The compact companion is a required product surface, not an additional depth
track. Pointing accuracy, broad GIMP depth, and other Clacky subsystems are
deferred unless the narrow manual gates reveal a concrete defect.
