# Three-minute demo script

## 0:00–0:25 — Problem and activation

Show the layered promotional graphic in foreground GIMP. Press `Ctrl+Alt+Space` once. Point out the persistent Listening indicator and say that PointPilot is general-purpose by architecture while GIMP is its first verified actuation environment.

## 0:25–0:55 — Teach

Say: “What is this panel and why are there multiple copies of my image?”

Expected: PointPilot captures live GIMP, explains the Layers panel, and places its overlay around the relevant live area without moving the system cursor.

## 0:55–1:20 — Guide

Say: “How do I make the product stand out without changing the headline?”

Expected: one grounded step, not a detached checklist. PointPilot waits for the next turn and can inspect the updated screen.

## 1:20–1:50 — Switch to Act

Say: “Actually, do it for me.”

Expected: visible GIMP mouse/keyboard actions from GPT-5.6 Computer Use. The original goal and constraint to protect the headline remain in the task.

## 1:50–2:15 — Interrupt

During execution say: “Wait, do not move it. Keep it on the left and make the shadow subtler.”

Expected: the old revision stops before its next action, PointPilot captures current GIMP, preserves completed safe steps, and replans to the correction. Demonstrate Escape as the physical stop only in a separate run so it does not consume this correction.

## 2:15–2:40 — Complete and verify

Say: “Change the subtitle to ‘Built for Focus’ and export it as a PNG to C:\Users\<user>\Pictures\pointpilot-built-for-focus.png.”

Expected: a keyboard-focusable confirmation names the exact operation/path and overwrite possibility. Confirm it. PointPilot must verify the final screenshot and exact file existence before saying it is done.

## 2:40–3:00 — Technical close

Show the exported PNG, state that GIMP is operated through visible UI rather than an API or plug-in, and name the harness responsibilities: Realtime voice, interruption/revision, target capture, guarded input, confirmation, overlay, and verification.
